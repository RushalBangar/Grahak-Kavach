Privacy Policy

Data Privacy & Protection

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect scan images and ingredient text locally on your device. This data is only transmitted to our servers if you explicitly choose to file a complaint.

2. Identity Verification

Aadhaar numbers and other sensitive IDs are never stored in our database. We only record a verified/unverified status flag returned by the authentication gateway.

3. DPDP Act Compliance

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, your data is used strictly for the stated purpose of regulatory compliance reporting. You have the right to request deletion of your complaint data at any time.
