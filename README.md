# 🛡️ Grahak Kavach (ग्राहक कवच)
**AI-Powered Packaged Commodity Compliance & Food Safety Platform**

*Developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 — Student Innovation Track*

## 📌 Overview
Grahak Kavach is a dual-engine compliance platform designed to bring absolute transparency to packaged commodities. By utilizing computer vision and AI-driven text extraction, consumers can scan a product label once and instantly receive two vital insights:
1. **Legal Metrology Compliance:** Verifies if the product adheres to the Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011 (MRP, Net Quantity, Manufacturer details).
2. **Food Safety Analysis:** Cross-checks extracted ingredients against a database of harmful/restricted substances, providing a Nutri-Score style health rating.

If a violation is found, the platform enables users to file **verified, evidence-backed complaints** (using offline Aadhaar QR/DigiLocker verification) that are automatically routed to the appropriate enforcement agency (FSSAI or Dept. of Consumer Affairs).

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Single-Scan Dual Analysis:** One photo powers both legal and nutritional rule engines.
* **Ingredient & Allergen Scanner:** Flags harmful chemicals and personal allergens hidden in complex label text.
* **Evidence-Backed Reporting:** Eliminates false complaints by requiring geo-tagged photos and strict identity verification.
* **Enforcement Dashboard:** A lightweight portal for Legal Metrology officers to log inspections and maintain public shop-compliance histories.
